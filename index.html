07 / 08GRADUATION PROJECT

PROJECT

01

REVIVING WASTED ENERGY IN RAILWAY CARS

ENERGY RECOVERY & CONVERSION SYSTEM
↗

The project explores recovering part of a railway car's kinetic energy as electrical energy for auxiliary use. In the model, a motor simulates train movement by turning the wheels; a belt or gear train drives a dynamo, and the generated electricity is stored in a battery. Displays monitor dynamo voltage and battery charge. Fans mounted above the car generate additional energy from airflow.
